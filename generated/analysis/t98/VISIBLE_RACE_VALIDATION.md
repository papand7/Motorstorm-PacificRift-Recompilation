## Why the images are black (open)
- **VERIFIED:** the dump copies the swap-chain backbuffer after `guest_fb_present` and `composite_present` (`rsx_d3d12_backend.c:7472–7520`). The same exe dumped a correct loading screen in T96I, so the capture mechanism works.
- **PARTIAL:** the dump rate (about 8.9 qualifying passes/s) matches the render-pass rate, not the guest-frame rate. Early-replay passes with ≥ 200 draws therefore also count as qualifying, so some dumps may come from non-presenting passes. That does not explain 35 of 35 black images.
- **HYPOTHESIS (unranked):**
  1. The race scene renders offscreen and reaches the display through a composite or post-process pass (exposure / tone map) whose output is black. This matches the unresolved "brightens to white, then goes black" frontend symptom in `AGENTS.md`.
  2. The 20% `position_attrib_disabled` drops remove scene geometry in the offscreen targets.
  3. The `keep_targets` / `flushed_display` handling across early replays leaves the display target cleared at Present.
- No workaround was tried.
