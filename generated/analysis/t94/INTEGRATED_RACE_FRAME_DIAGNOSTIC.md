## A. Is the geometry valid? PARTIAL (leaning valid)
**The sampled draw decodes consistently (VERIFIED):**
- **Header:** `0x40141824` is a non-increment header with 5 words for `DRAW_INDEX_ARRAY` (0x1824), as libgcm emits it.
- **Data word:** `0xFF00AE92` decodes as count 256, first 44690. The re-read matches memory.
- **Index array:** `idx_dma=0x10` means 16-bit indices in local memory.
- **Primitive restart:** active with 0xFFFF. It is only counted when `ps3_prim_restart_active` holds.
- **Indices:** the 205 non-restart indices span 4594..4797 (204 vertices). Restarts recur every 5 indices, so this is 51 quads drawn as 4-index strips.
- **Attribute 0:** float3, stride 24.

**Expansion arithmetic:**
- The VP path emits a triangle list: 51 × 2 triangles × 3 = about 306 vertices written.
- `vp_reserve` asks for `max_tris*3 = 768`. It ignores restarts, matching T95's restart over-reservation finding.
- So `frame_want` (590,220) overstates demand, and the frame-end grow decision is sized on that over-reservation.
- But `used` counts vertices actually written, and it reached the full 393,216 by draw 967. **Real writes alone overflow the buffer.**

**Not shown:** vertex positions, and every draw other than this one. That is why A is PARTIAL.

## B. Is vertex-buffer growth responsible for the stall? BLOCKED in this run
- **This run:** with `VPVB_GROW_AT_FRAME_END=1` there is no entry-time copy. The run ended before frame 2133's `render_frame` returned, so there is no completion, no phase timing and no Present outcome.

## C. Smallest justified correction (not implemented)
- **Proven defect:** strip/fan `want` ignores restarts.
- **Fix:** reserve `3 × (non-restart indices − 2 × segments)`, or reserve after the expansion loop, in `upload_strip_vp_indexed`.
- **Tests:** a restart-heavy strip (this exact pattern: 256 indices, 51 restarts) must give `want == written`, plus a capacity-boundary case.
- **Limit:** this does not remove the overflow, because writes alone exceed 393,216.
