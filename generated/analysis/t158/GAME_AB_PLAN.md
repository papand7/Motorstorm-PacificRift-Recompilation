## 4. Decision rules

- **B is faster** only if its mean light-scene solver + collide execution (kind 39) and its PPU Havok wait are each lower by more than twice the A-to-A spread.
- **Expected** (HYPOTHESIS from offline): about −5 ms of solver/collide execution per physics frame (4.4–7.2 ms). The DMA/atomic share, not visible offline, can only shrink that.
- **Correctness gates for B** (any failure rejects it):
  - per-race-frame draw and vertex counts match A at least as well as A matches A;
  - same task count per chain;
  - same scene-change frame;
  - no crash, hang or new error line.
- **FPS.** A lower Havok wait raises FPS only where the frame waits on Havok, mainly the light scene. The heavy scene is RSX-bound (T157's area). No FPS claim is made from offline numbers.
